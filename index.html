How this works

Our poll average has no smoothing and no house effects. On each day, take each pollster's latest poll fielded in the previous 14 days (30 days before the first round, when polls were sparser), compute Flávio's two-way share, flávio ÷ (lula + flávio), and average them equally. It needs at least three pollsters. Weekly trackers count once. The series restarts after the first round on Oct 4, because earlier polls were hypothetical matchups.

Plano Político's average is their national-only aggregate in valid votes, which uses their own weighting. The model is Plano Político's presidential model. Before Oct 4 its numbers include the chance of winning outright in the first round and other runoff pairings, so Lula and Flávio don't add up to 100% there. Days before Aug 15 are their reconstruction. The market is the daily price of the "Yes" share on Polymarket's Brazil presidential election market.
